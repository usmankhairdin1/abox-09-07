## Root cause

It also gives no identity check at all for the three "writable" pages, and reports no offender names.

This is the only failing item: every B6 pattern check above it (pattern inventory, source signature, nested-component resolution, component-property ids, no-duplicate/no-new-set, layout, stroke, state, interaction, deferred/rejected candidates, idempotency) and the B0 seven-page/order assertion at 3676–3679 pass.
